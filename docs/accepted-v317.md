# BLACKOUT v3.17 accepted low and informational findings

The v3.16 findings (`docs/accepted-v316.md`) are carried below, with the early-answer wording corrected
for reproduced import audit `1caeadee` (see `ADAPTATION.md`). Pre-token audit 339b6b19's one medium and three
low findings are **fixed** in v3.17, not accepted (`report.md` §v3.17, `test/AuditV317.t.sol`); its info 5–8 are
accepted here. The accepted items from the L1 launch judge are added at the end. The audit's own text for info 5–8
and the judge's wording were not available to this job; each line states the finding from the code it names.

## Fixed in v3.17 (for the record, not accepted)

- 339b6b19 medium, Enhancement re-roll: an attempt whose 256-block window lapsed is settled at once as the worst outcome of its level (no success; levels 1–3 keep their level, 4+ lose one, any burn chance burns); never re-armed.
- 339b6b19 low, GridOperator fallback: a lapsed hash window re-arms the event to a fresh future block and keeps the submitted plan; no seed computable before the epoch is ever used, and `advanceEpoch` defers until the fresh hash is in.
- 339b6b19 low, `Economy.unlock` while stale: an expired lock's principal is always returned; the weight resync is skipped while no epoch is live or the account is behind.
- 339b6b19 low, destroyed item keeps weight: a destroyed item removed by `process` leaves the committed weight at the next epoch (recomputed from the loadout once the farm is caught up), so global and district hash stop counting it.
- Review e11b8d0f medium (ported from launch 1593 commit 1e4490923f20a389ecb6be136121f4eea65db3b1): the `lastFarmDaily` price basis rises at once to a higher settled yield and otherwise decays by 1/96 per settled epoch; pro-rata entry epochs are scaled to a full-epoch equivalent.

## Carried from v3.16

- Accepted — RewardPool shortfall: repair an accounting shortfall with a plain token transfer to RewardPool; `donate` is not the shortfall-repair path.
- Accepted — Exact-input swaps only: V4Market intentionally rejects exact-output swaps; routers and integrations must request exact input with a minimum output.
- Accepted — `spot()` bounds: zero/uninitialized, underflowed, or prices above `1e40` tokens per ETH are unsupported and revert; operators must keep the launched pool inside these bounds.
- Accepted — Fixed oracle signer/endpoints: the Robinhood protocol addresses and signer are compiled into this version, with no owner rotation; protocol migration requires a separately reviewed source release.
- Accepted — Answers between job A and job B: after L3 binds, regulator answers may be stored immediately; stream-0 event answers require `FarmRewards` (role 1) to be registered and the attested epoch to match. Before role 1 exists, stream-0 intake reverts `NotConfigured` and stores nothing. Keepers defer execution until all L4 modules are registered and wiring completes; accepted answers remain subject to their validity deadlines.
- Accepted — Future-block randomness: pack, event, enhancement, and lottery entropy uses future native L2 blocks, with capture/rearm rules and sequencer/withholding risk; it is not verifiable randomness. (v3.17: enhancement attempts no longer re-arm, they lapse to the worst outcome; grid events re-arm instead of using a fixed seed.)
- Accepted — Reserved Params keys: the reader-less keys listed in `docs/params-keys-v313.md` remain reserved and have no gameplay effect; valid proposals may still consume their cooldown.
- Accepted — M-2 matching twin: after the Desk/hook/token/configuration checks, a matching factory-deployed twin can still win the one-time bind and orphan the honest graph; this is accepted as griefing under the pinned game rules, mitigated by binding immediately after L3 and checking before forwarding. (v3.16: a twin can no longer bind at all, since only the pinned binder may call `bind`; the line is kept because the binder key itself could still choose a proven twin, see `docs/m2-trust-assumption.md`.)
- Accepted — 031c93c4 low 2, `GameRegistry.register` prefill by an identical copy: `register` is permissionless and accepts any factory coordinates whose CREATE2 result is the pinned module code constructed with this registry, so a byte-identical copy launched through the ProjectFactory could take a role before the keeper registers the recorded job A/B module; the copy runs the same pinned code against the same registry, no authority or funds move, and the keeper registers every role immediately after each launch is recorded and verifies `module(role)` against the recorded addresses before any further wiring.
- Accepted — 031c93c4 low 3, `Season.rollover` before `finalize`: once a week's claim deadline passes, its unclaimed pot can be rolled into the current week whether or not that week was finalized, so a week nobody finalized rolls its whole pot forward instead of paying its winners; mitigated by the keeper finalizing every week (finalize, then rollover, in its weekly pass), and a rollover only moves the pot to later players, never out of the game.
- Accepted — 031c93c4 low 4, `FeeRouter.executeSells` sweep rollback: the heartbeat-excess sweep to RewardPool at the top of `executeSells` shares the transaction with the sell, so a sell that reverts (unfillable minimum, market failure) also rolls the sweep back; the sweep repeats on the next successful call and no value is lost.
- Accepted — 031c93c4 info 5, `FarmRewards._credit`: a same-epoch scrap/award credit back-dates the maturity of the later settlement credit for that epoch by up to one epoch (`if (v.amount == 0 || at < v.at) v.at = uint64(at);`). Settlement credits pass `at = e.end`, but scrap and award credit the current epoch with `at = block.timestamp`, which is earlier than `e.end`. When a player scraps during epoch N and N is settled later, the settlement credit merges into the same slot and inherits the scrap timestamp, so its 7-day tax age starts up to 6 hours early. `taxBps` falls 1500 bps over 7 days, so the mispricing is at most about 54 bps of the settlement credit, in the player favour and self-triggered; reserved accounting is unchanged (`pending == matured + sum(vintages)` still holds). `test/AuditV314.t.sol` `testLaterSameEpochCreditKeepsEarlierTaxVintage` shows the earliest-timestamp merge is intended for two awards in one epoch. Launch judge: would not block. Fix if wanted: pass `epochs[epoch].end` as `at` for scrap and award credits (FarmRewards pin only). Accepted as is.
- Accepted — 031c93c4 info 6, runbook says the deploy-gate tool accepts launch documents, but the tool only `JSON.parse`s its arguments and crashes on `launch-v39.md` (`tools/check_deploy_gate.mjs`). The manual check of all 19 constructors in launch-v39.md finds no IMD protocol address, and the tool passes on `docs/launch-v314-plan.json` (19 constructors). Risk is only operational. Launch judge: would not block. Resolved in the runbook since v3.15 (the JSON plan is the tool's only input); the tool is unchanged.
- Accepted — Binder liveness: bind is a one-time call by the pinned binder 0x6d51…d007; if that key is lost before binding, the Desk can never bind and the endowment stays in the escrow; the binder cannot bypass any proof.

## Pre-token audit 339b6b19, info 5–8

- Accepted — 339b6b19 info 5, `Civic.claimWar` rounding dust: a war pot is split by integer division across the winning district's claimants, so a few wei per split stay in the contract instead of being paid out; bounded by the number of claimants, never leaves the game, and no claimant can take another's share. No pin change.
- Accepted — 339b6b19 info 6, no max-cost bound on `PackSale.openPack` / `FarmCore.buyTokenPack`: the token price is read at execution from the last settled yield, so a buyer whose transaction lands after a settlement pays the price of that block, not the quoted one. The wallet path is bounded by the buyer's token approval and the ETH zap by its minimum output; `roomQuote`-style `maxCost` arguments exist for enhancement only. Accepted as a UX limit: the UI shows the current `price()` and the approval caps the charge.
- Accepted — 339b6b19 info 7, unused minter/caller permissions: some pinned modules are allowed callers of `FarmItems.mint` / `maintain` / `destroy` (Season, Civic, Economy) on paths the current code never exercises; every allowed caller is a pinned module of the same registry, so no outside authority exists. Left as is to keep the fourteen pins minimal.
- Accepted — 339b6b19 info 8, dead fields: storage fields written but never read (for example `PackSale.Commit.expired`, written for `PackOpened` only, and pre-v3.5 Farm fields kept for layout) cost gas but have no gameplay or security effect. Left as is; removing them changes pins without a behaviour change.

## L1 launch judge, accepted

- Accepted — `PackSale.buyPacksFor` gifting: anyone can buy packs for another address, which registers that farm and credits its paid-pack count and XP; a gift cannot move funds out of the recipient, only in, and the recipient's items stay soulbound.
- Accepted — One-shot escrow: `EndowmentEscrow.forward` runs once to the proven RewardPool of the bound registry; there is no retry, owner or recovery path, which is the intended no-admin design, mitigated by the binder proofs and the runbook check before forwarding.
- Accepted — Festival band: the Desk clamps attested severities to its 5000–7000 band (the fallback table keeps 10000), so a Festival plan can never reach full severity; accepted as the designed ceiling for reserved kinds.
- Accepted — `Economy._insert` 33-tie lock griefing: 32 equal-amount locks at the head of a district make a deeper insertion revert `InvalidValue` atomically (runbook wiring item 10); a larger stake that belongs earlier or an earlier lock's exit makes insertion possible, and no funds are at risk.
- Accepted — Stream-0 answers before role 1 (corrected after reproducing import audit `1caeadee`): `submit` and oracle callbacks revert `NotConfigured` while `FarmRewards` (role 1) is unregistered. The attestation is neither consumed nor stored. A relayer may re-present the same signed bytes after registration only if the current slot, epoch, issued-at window and expiry still permit it. A paid backup may already have spent its oracle fee: its failed 200,000-gas callback leaves `pending[id]` intact, and `askedSlotPlusOne` prevents another backup in that slot. `clearExpired` removes the pending entry but neither refunds the fee nor reopens the slot. If the signed bytes remain available and fresh they can still be relayed with `submit`. Register and verify role 1 before stream-0 submission or backup purchase, and defer execution until wiring completes. Contract source and creation hashes are unchanged.
